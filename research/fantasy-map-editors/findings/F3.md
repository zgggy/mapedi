# F3: VTT and worldbuilding map integration

## Findings

### [1] Foundry VTT treats one Scene model as both battle map and world/regional map, with grid scale unit free-text and per-scene grid type including four hex orientations plus gridless.
- quote: "Scenes may depict a variety of settings ranging from world or regional maps all the way down to small buildings or dungeons." / "For battle maps this may be on the order of feet or meters, for a large region map each individual grid space could represent miles or kilometers." / Grid Types: "Gridless … Square … Hexagonal Columns, Odd … Hexagonal Columns, Even … Hexagonal Rows, Odd … Hexagonal Rows, Even"
- url: https://foundryvtt.com/article/scenes/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (docs current as of 2026 fetch)
- confidence: high

### [2] Foundry fog-of-war is an optional per-user Fog Exploration mode on the Scene, with configurable unexplored image and explored/unexplored color tints rather than only a binary mask.
- quote: "If this setting is enabled, fog-of-war exploration will be tracked on a per-User basis and stored to the database as the Scene is explored." / "Fog of War Image … Fog of War Unexplored Color … Fog of War Explored Color"
- url: https://foundryvtt.com/article/scenes/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [3] Foundry Map Notes are pin/POI objects that bind canvas coordinates to Journal Entry or Journal Entry Page, and their visibility is gated by both fog exploration and journal permission levels (None / Limited / Observer / Owner).
- quote: "Foundry Virtual Tabletop supports the creation of Map Notes that you can easily place on a Scene" / "By default, Map Notes are obscured by Fog on scenes with Fog Exploration and will only appear when they are within the field of vision for a controlled Token." / "If a player has \"Limited\" permissions, they will be able to see the position of the Map Note and its assigned label, but they will not be able to open it."
- url: https://foundryvtt.com/article/map-notes/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [4] Foundry implements Notion/Obsidian-style wiki linking in journals via dynamic document links (@DocumentType[id], @UUID, custom labels) plus drag-and-drop header anchors into long pages.
- quote: "The syntax to create a dynamic link is as follows: @DocumentType[Document Name] @DocumentType[Document ID]{Custom Text Label}" / "the ability to provide a link to a specific header within a journal entry … generates a dynamic link which, when clicked, will automatically open that Journal Entry to the specified header"
- url: https://foundryvtt.com/article/journal/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [5] Foundry measurement separates distance measurement from movement-cost measurement, with multi-waypoint rulers that auto-compute Euclidean elevation difference; area is handled by Measured Templates (circle/cone/rectangle/ray) that highlight grid/hex spaces.
- quote: "The Measure Distance ruler measures *distance*, while the Token Drag Measurement ruler measures *movement cost*." / "If waypoints are at different elevations, the Euclidean difference between them is automatically calculated." / "If your scene is using the Grid or Hex grid types it also shows you all the spaces affected."
- url: https://foundryvtt.com/article/measurement/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (V13-era docs; screenshots dated 2025-02/2025-03)
- confidence: high

### [6] Foundry Scene Regions encode semantic map zones (indoors, stairs, traps, difficult terrain) as shapes+behaviors with events (Token Enters/Exits, turn/round hooks) and behaviors including Teleport Token across scenes and Modify Movement Cost multipliers from 0 to 5 in 0.25 steps.
- quote: "Scene Regions allow you to define these interesting parts of your map so that they do remarkable things or behave in certain ways." / "set a multiplier (from 0 to 5 in increments of 0.25) on the \"cost\" of movement through a scene region" / "the destination region can even be in a different scene!"
- url: https://foundryvtt.com/article/scene-regions/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (feature diagrams dated 2024-04/05; trap screenshots 2025-03)
- confidence: high

### [7] LegendKeeper positions maps as a first-class Google-Maps-like layer over the wiki: indefinitely nestable maps, pins linked to wiki pages, image pins, journey tracking, and GM-only hidden treasures/secrets.
- quote: "Your world map isn't just an image. Nest your interactive maps, zoom from continents to crypts, and link every landmark to its wiki page." / Map features list: "Pin locations … Track journeys … Nest huge maps … Hide treasures"
- url: https://www.legendkeeper.com/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (site live 2026-09; ENnies Gold link is 2024 nominees)
- confidence: high

### [8] LegendKeeper explicitly is not a VTT and expects map tools to integrate by side-by-side use with Foundry/Roll20/Fantasy Grounds rather than replacing tabletop combat maps.
- quote: "LegendKeeper isn't a VTT—it's your worldbuilding website and home base. Use it alongside Foundry, Roll20, Fantasy Grounds, or any other VTT you love."
- url: https://www.legendkeeper.com/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [9] LegendKeeper timelines (marked NEW on the 2026 landing page) bundle fantasy calendars with event plots, moon phases, eras, lineages, and parallel storylines — i.e. calendar is expected as world-state infrastructure, not just a GM utility.
- quote: "Timelines NEW … From ancient conflicts to future empires: catalog events to build a consistent, immersive history for any world." / Timeline features: "Observe moon phases … Chronicle eras … Fantasy Calendars … Trace lineages … Track parallel storylines"
- url: https://www.legendkeeper.com/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (feature marked NEW as of 2026-09 site)
- confidence: high

### [10] Roll20 markets Dynamic Lighting & Fog of War as a core VTT immersion feature ("Reveal maps as players explore") and pairs it with free integrated Dungeon Scrawl mapmaking; product claims 15+ million players and 1,200+ game systems.
- quote: "Dynamic Lighting & Fog of War — Reveal maps as players explore (immersion advantage)" / "Free Mapmaking with Dungeon Scrawl — Create maps in seconds with integrated tools" / "Join 15+ million players"
- url: https://roll20.net/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (site live 2026-09)
- confidence: high

### [11] Fantasy Calendar is a standalone calendar engine expected to plug into wikis via external embed and Discord, and to keep multiple planetary/societal calendars synchronized — the integration pattern map tools must match or absorb.
- quote: "Want to embed your calendar in a website or wiki for your players? Just generate and paste some code." / "Calendar linking — Have different calendars for different planets/societies? Keep them in sync, automatically." / "Control your calendar and share it with your players right from Discord"
- url: https://www.fantasy-calendar.com/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (© 2026 Fantasy Computerworks Ltd.)
- confidence: high

### [12] Fantasy Calendar's engine expectations for world maps' time layer: custom month counts, leap-day interval systems, non-24h days (e.g. 28h × 120min), moon cycles, seasons, eras, climate-based weather, and event conditions.
- quote: "intercalaries, leap days, moons cycles, seasons, eras, and more!" / "Your world spins slowly, with 28 hours per day and 120 minutes per hour? Done." / "A moon that turns purple during summer, on Fridays, only when the other two moons are new moons? Yep, you can do that, too."
- url: https://www.fantasy-calendar.com/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [13] Notion-style wiki linking baseline (the bar worldbuilders expect in notes): type @ to link pages, Synced Blocks for one-source multi-embed, and Verification for stale-doc trust — plus sidebar/search as primary navigation.
- quote: "Everything is connected in your workspace — just type @ to link your notes or roadmap in a doc." / "Introducing Synced Blocks — Keep content updated, automatically." / "Verification — Never wonder \"is this the latest version?\""
- url: https://www.notion.com/product/wikis
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (product page live 2026-09)
- confidence: high

### [14] World Anvil itself treats battle maps as a distinct map class from world maps and outsources tactical battle-map authoring (e.g. DungeonFog), while planning workflow expects POIs to be listed before drawing — evidence of a world-map ↔ battle-map pipeline gap in wiki tools.
- quote: "battle maps are only one kind of map you might need for your campaign." / "Points of interest: Decide on any notable locations or landmarks that will be present on the map" / "uploading it to your VTT of choice depending on your style of play!"
- url: https://blog.worldanvil.com/mapmaking/create-great-dd-battle-maps-even-if-you-cant-draw/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2022-12-23 (pre-2024 window; used only for the battle-map vs world-map framing still current in WA mapmaking category)
- confidence: medium

- quote: "200K+ CREATORS 180K+ WorLDS 26M+ pages created 2M+ Pins dropped" / "Ennies Gold winner" / Pro: "Unlimited maps and asset sizes Unlimited timelines and events Unlimited whiteboards"
